## The claim

**Mutations in PfDHFR are associated with higher rates of antimalarial treatment failure, consistently across studies**

## What it rests on

> [!info] About this gene
> **PfDHFR** is dihydrofolate reductase, the parasite enzyme that the drug pyrimethamine blocks.

6 findings in the System Public Graph relate PfDHFR to whether malaria treatment failed, drawn from 4 studies published 2003 to 2015. Of the 6 findings whose direction can be determined, **6 point the same way** (100%) and 0 point the other way.
