## The claim

**Mutations in Pfmdr1 are associated with higher rates of antimalarial treatment failure, though a minority of findings disagree**

## What it rests on

> [!info] About this gene
> **Pfmdr1** is the malaria parasite's multidrug-resistance gene, which affects how well several antimalarial drugs work.

9 findings in the System Public Graph relate Pfmdr1 to whether malaria treatment failed, drawn from 7 studies published 2004 to 2018. Of the 9 findings whose direction can be determined, **8 point the same way** (89%) and 1 points the other way.
