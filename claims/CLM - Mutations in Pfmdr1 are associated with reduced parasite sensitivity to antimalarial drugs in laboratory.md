## The claim

**Mutations in Pfmdr1 are associated with reduced parasite sensitivity to antimalarial drugs in laboratory testing — but the published evidence is contested**

## What it rests on

> [!info] About this gene
> **Pfmdr1** is the malaria parasite's multidrug-resistance gene, which affects how well several antimalarial drugs work.

4 findings in the System Public Graph relate Pfmdr1 to how well antimalarial drugs killed the parasite in the laboratory, drawn from 4 studies published 2003 to 2021. Of the 3 findings whose direction can be determined, **2 point the same way** (67%) and 1 points the other way.
