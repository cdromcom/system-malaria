## The claim

**Resistance-mutation prevalence in Pfmdr1 rises with drug pressure and over time across the settings studied, though a minority of findings disagree**

## What it rests on

> [!info] About this gene
> **Pfmdr1** is the malaria parasite's multidrug-resistance gene, which affects how well several antimalarial drugs work.

44 findings in the System Public Graph relate Pfmdr1 to how common the resistance mutation was, drawn from 22 studies published 2005 to 2021. Of the 43 findings whose direction can be determined, **32 point the same way** (74%) and 11 point the other way.

> [!warning] How far this claim can be pushed
> The direction above is inferred from each statistic's sign against its null value — 1 for ratios, 0 for differences and correlations — not from any direction field, because the graph has none. The findings use different statistic types and cannot be pooled into a single effect size, so this claim asserts a **direction and its consistency**, not a magnitude. Which variable is cause and which is effect is also unrecorded. See [[CVT - The graph records no effect direction]].
