## The claim

**Mutations in Pfkelch13 are positively associated with a range of other malaria measures — but the published evidence is contested**

## What it rests on

> [!info] About this gene
> **Pfkelch13** is the malaria parasite gene whose mutations cause partial resistance to artemisinin, the backbone of modern malaria treatment.

5 findings in the System Public Graph relate Pfkelch13 to other malaria outcomes, drawn from 4 studies published 2016 to 2018. Of the 5 findings whose direction can be determined, **3 point the same way** (60%) and 2 point the other way.
