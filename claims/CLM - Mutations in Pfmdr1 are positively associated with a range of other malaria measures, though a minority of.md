## The claim

**Mutations in Pfmdr1 are positively associated with a range of other malaria measures, though a minority of findings disagree**

## What it rests on

> [!info] About this gene
> **Pfmdr1** is the malaria parasite's multidrug-resistance gene, which affects how well several antimalarial drugs work.

32 findings in the System Public Graph relate Pfmdr1 to other malaria outcomes, drawn from 19 studies published 1997 to 2021. Of the 32 findings whose direction can be determined, **26 point the same way** (81%) and 6 point the other way.

> [!warning] How far this claim can be pushed
> The direction above is inferred from each statistic's sign against its null value — 1 for ratios, 0 for differences and correlations — not from any direction field, because the graph has none. The findings use different statistic types and cannot be pooled into a single effect size, so this claim asserts a **direction and its consistency**, not a magnitude.
