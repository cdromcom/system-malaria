## The claim

**Mutations in Pfkelch13 are associated with higher rates of antimalarial treatment failure, though a minority of findings disagree**

## What it rests on

> [!info] About this gene
> **Pfkelch13** is the malaria parasite gene whose mutations cause partial resistance to artemisinin, the backbone of modern malaria treatment.

5 findings in the System Public Graph relate Pfkelch13 to whether malaria treatment failed, drawn from 5 studies published 2015 to 2021. Of the 5 findings whose direction can be determined, **4 point the same way** (80%) and 1 points the other way.
