## The claim

**Mutations in PfDHFR are positively associated with a range of other malaria measures, consistently across studies**

## What it rests on

> [!info] About this gene
> **PfDHFR** is dihydrofolate reductase, the parasite enzyme that the drug pyrimethamine blocks.

8 findings in the System Public Graph relate PfDHFR to other malaria outcomes, drawn from 5 studies published 2010 to 2018. Of the 8 findings whose direction can be determined, **8 point the same way** (100%) and 0 point the other way.
