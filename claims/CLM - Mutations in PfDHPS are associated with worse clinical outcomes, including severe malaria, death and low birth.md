## The claim

**Mutations in PfDHPS are associated with worse clinical outcomes, including severe malaria, death and low birth weight — but the published evidence is contested**

## What it rests on

> [!info] About this gene
> **PfDHPS** is dihydropteroate synthase, the parasite enzyme that the drug sulfadoxine blocks.
