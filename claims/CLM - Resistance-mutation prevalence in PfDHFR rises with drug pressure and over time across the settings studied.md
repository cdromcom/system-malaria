## The claim

**Resistance-mutation prevalence in PfDHFR rises with drug pressure and over time across the settings studied, though a minority of findings disagree**

## What it rests on

> [!info] About this gene
> **PfDHFR** is dihydrofolate reductase, the parasite enzyme that the drug pyrimethamine blocks.

20 findings in the System Public Graph relate PfDHFR to how common the resistance mutation was, drawn from 13 studies published 2003 to 2024. Of the 20 findings whose direction can be determined, **15 point the same way** (75%) and 5 point the other way.
