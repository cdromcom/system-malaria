## The claim

**Mutations in Pfkelch13 are associated with slower clearance of malaria parasites after treatment, consistently across studies**

## What it rests on

> [!info] About this gene
> **Pfkelch13** is the malaria parasite gene whose mutations cause partial resistance to artemisinin, the backbone of modern malaria treatment.

7 findings in the System Public Graph relate Pfkelch13 to how fast malaria parasites cleared from the blood, drawn from 5 studies published 2017 to 2023. Of the 7 findings whose direction can be determined, **7 point the same way** (100%) and 0 point the other way.
