## The claim

**Mutations in PfDHPS are associated with higher rates of antimalarial treatment failure, consistently across studies**

## What it rests on

> [!info] About this gene
> **PfDHPS** is dihydropteroate synthase, the parasite enzyme that the drug sulfadoxine blocks.

5 findings in the System Public Graph relate PfDHPS to whether malaria treatment failed, drawn from 3 studies published 2008 to 2015. Of the 5 findings whose direction can be determined, **5 point the same way** (100%) and 0 point the other way.
