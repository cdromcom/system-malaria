## The claim

**Resistance-mutation prevalence in PfDHPS rises with drug pressure and over time across the settings studied — but the published evidence is contested**

## What it rests on

> [!info] About this gene
> **PfDHPS** is dihydropteroate synthase, the parasite enzyme that the drug sulfadoxine blocks.

23 findings in the System Public Graph relate PfDHPS to how common the resistance mutation was, drawn from 15 studies published 2008 to 2024. Of the 23 findings whose direction can be determined, **15 point the same way** (65%) and 8 point the other way.
