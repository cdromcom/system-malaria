## Statement

Variables found per gene in the API sweep: PfCRT 1,070 (mostly substring noise), Pfmdr1 436, other markers 364, PfDHFR 113, PfDHPS 59, Pfkelch13 30, plasmepsin 11. After filtering, the evidence notes in this vault split as Pfmdr1 90, PfCRT 77, PfDHPS 30, PfDHFR 29, Pfkelch13 21, other 6, plasmepsin 1.

## Why it limits the evidence

Pfkelch13 is the gene behind partial resistance to artemisinin, the drug family at the core of modern malaria treatment. Resistance has spread through Southeast Asia and has now reached East Africa, which is the reason this challenge exists — and it has the least evidence in the graph. Twenty-one findings is too few to replay a trajectory. A working prototype should be built on Pfmdr1 or on the DHFR/DHPS pair, with Pfkelch13 shown as the case where the method correctly fires too rarely. That contrast is itself a finding about where surveillance evidence is missing.
