## In plain terms

> [!info] What this finding says
> A study published in 2011 reported a statistical link between **isolates with two or more DNNND repeats in the pfnhe1 microsatellite ms4760 locus** and **quinine susceptibility**.
> Quinine susceptibility differed by 382 between isolates with two or more DNNND repeats in the pfnhe1 and isolates with zero or one DNNND repeats (mean difference unstandardized), p = 0.0146.
