## In plain terms

> [!info] What this finding says
> A study published in 2007 reported a statistical link between **children living in villages in which insecticide-treated curtains had been used for 6-8 years** and **clearance of parasites with the pfcrt-76T allele**.
> Clearance of parasites with the pfcrt-76T allele differed by 10.1 between children living in villages in which insecticide-treated curtains and children living in unprotected villages (mean difference unstandardized), p = 0.01.
