## The claim

**A directional assertion someone could disagree with.** Not a count. Follow the
living-evidence-synthesis style: "Mutations in PfCRT are associated with higher rates of
antimalarial treatment failure, consistently across studies."

The trailing qualifier is set by how much the underlying findings agree:

| Agreement | Qualifier | `TruthValue` |
|---|---|---|
| ≥ 90% | ", consistently across studies" | the agreement proportion |
| 70–89% | ", though a minority of findings disagree" | the agreement proportion |
| < 70% | " — but the published evidence is contested" | the agreement proportion |

## What it rests on

> [!info] About this gene

Counts, studies, publication window, direction and consistency, how many are confirmed in the
curated dataset, and which statistic types are involved.

> [!warning] How far this claim can be pushed
> Direction is inferred from each statistic's sign against its null value, not from a direction
> field — the graph has none. Mixed statistic types cannot be pooled, so the claim asserts a
> direction and its consistency, never a magnitude.
