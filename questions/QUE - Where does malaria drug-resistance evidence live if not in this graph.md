## The question

**Where does malaria drug-resistance evidence live, if not in the published statistical literature this graph covers?**

## Why it matters

The graph only holds findings that look like statistical relationships extracted from journal articles. Much of what the world knows about antimalarial resistance is not shaped that way, which explains why coverage of the newest and most urgent markers is so thin.

## Where we have got to

> [!info] Current answer
> The main sources outside the graph are MalariaGEN, a genomic surveillance programme that sequences parasites directly; WWARN, which pools drug-efficacy results across studies; the World Health Organization's annual World Malaria Report; and national therapeutic efficacy studies. None of these publish their results as the kind of extractable statistical sentence this graph is built from. This is also the subject of the hackathon's Explore Challenge 3.
