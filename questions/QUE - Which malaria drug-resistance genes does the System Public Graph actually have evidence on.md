## The question

**Which malaria drug-resistance genes does the System Public Graph actually hold evidence on, and how much?**

## Why it matters

Before building a monitoring tool you have to know whether the underlying data can support it. The System Public Graph gathers statistical findings from published research, so its coverage reflects what researchers have published and what the extraction pipeline managed to read — not what public-health programmes most need to know.

## Where we have got to

> [!info] Current answer
> Coverage is uneven and, awkwardly, thinnest where it matters most. See [[CVT - Pfkelch13 is the most important marker and the least covered]].
